## Three doors into Tage VC

Every deal we receive is tagged with a **thesis / path**. That is not bureaucracy — it ensures you talk to the right playbook from the first call.

### Launch

**Best for:** Pre-revenue or early-revenue founders building a new venture.

**You get:** Studio-style support, capital, GTM and ops leverage.

**Signals:** New product, new market entry, need for co-builders.

### Partner

**Best for:** Growth-stage companies with product-market fit seeking strategic leverage.

**You get:** Distribution, technology partnerships, structured investment.

**Signals:** Repeatable sales, partnership thesis, integration opportunity.

### Exit

**Best for:** Owners considering liquidity in the next 1–3 years.

**You get:** Confidential guidance on positioning, diligence, and buyer options.

**Signals:** Stable operations, succession planning, inbound or proactive sale interest.

## Not sure?

Select the closest fit on our form and explain your situation in notes. We reroute quickly if needed — the goal is momentum, not paperwork.
