## Beyond fundraising: the Partner path

Not every conversation with Tage Venture Capital is about starting something new. Many leaders come to us on the **Partner** path — when they have traction, a team, and a gap that a strategic relationship could fill.

Partnerships might include:

- **Distribution** — Access to customers or channels you cannot reach alone.
- **Technology** — Integrations, data, or platform capabilities that accelerate roadmap.
- **Capital** — Structured investment tied to partnership outcomes, not just valuation.

## How we evaluate Partner opportunities

We look for mutual leverage: your product or service should strengthen our portfolio or thesis, and our network should materially change your growth curve.

Signals we like:

- Recurring revenue or strong unit economics
- Clear ICP and reference customers
- A partnership hypothesis you can test in 90 days

## Working with Tage VC on Partner

Our deal-sourcing team tracks Partner deals separately from Launch and Exit. Expect diligence on fit, economics, and integration effort before term discussions.
