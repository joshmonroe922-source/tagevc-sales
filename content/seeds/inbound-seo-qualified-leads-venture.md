## SEO as infrastructure, not marketing fluff

For Tage Venture Capital, **search and content are the primary lead channel**. Founders and operators research options long before they fill out a form. If we are not visible in that moment, we lose deals we never knew existed.

## Our content principles

1. **Path-specific pages** — Launch, Partner, and Exit content answers different questions. We do not mash them into one generic "contact us."
2. **Depth over volume** — A smaller set of authoritative posts beats thin blogs no one reads.
3. **Internal linking** — Every article routes to a clear CTA and the right intake form.
4. **Deal sourcing + marketing alignment** — Blog and social content is managed in the same portal that runs deal flow and founder nurture.

## From click to conversation

When someone submits our form:

- A deal is created in **New**
- Josh gets an email alert
- A nurture sequence starts (thank-you, follow-up task, nurture reminder)
- Social posts can be scheduled to amplify the same themes

## What we publish

Thought leadership on venture studios, partnerships, and exits — plus practical guides for founders evaluating their next step.
